## 🎯 KKF Documentary Photography Direction

> **KKF photography should document, not advertise.**
>
> Images capture real-looking moments of young Kenyan creatives learning, experimenting, collaborating, and building their skills in Nairobi, Kenya. Photography feels candid, human, and observational, with natural light, authentic environments, realistic skin texture, restrained color, and imperfect composition.
>
> The visual language resembles a **documentary photo essay or nonprofit annual report**, rather than commercial stock photography.
>
> - **Human > polished**
> - **Authentic > perfect**
> - **Documentary > cinematic**
> - **Natural > heavily graded**
> - **Lived-in > staged**
> - **Real moments > posed moments**

### Standard Photographic DNA Style Block

```
Visual style: authentic documentary photography for a Kenyan youth creative foundation. Candid photojournalistic image captured during a real activity in Nairobi, Kenya. Natural available light, realistic exposure, soft natural shadows, neutral-to-slightly-muted color, accurate African skin tones, visible natural skin texture, realistic hair and clothing, ordinary lived-in environments, genuine human interaction, imperfect but intentional framing, realistic camera perspective, subtle depth of field, natural lens characteristics, documentary editorial photography, unposed moment, human and believable.

The photograph should feel like it was captured spontaneously by a photographer documenting a real nonprofit program for an annual report or photo essay.

Avoid: stock photography, commercial advertising photography, staged poses, everyone looking at camera, exaggerated smiles, perfect faces, perfect skin, glamour photography, cinematic movie lighting, dramatic rim lighting, HDR, excessive sharpness, excessive bokeh, oversaturation, orange-and-teal grading, glossy color grading, artificial studio lighting, futuristic environments, perfectly clean workspaces, perfectly arranged objects, unrealistic equipment, plastic-looking skin, distorted hands, duplicated people, AI-generated appearance.
```
